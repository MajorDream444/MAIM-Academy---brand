# Asset usage and approval

Major Dream Williams is the final approver for brand identity changes.

## Statuses

- reference: source material; not a production approval.
- draft: work in progress.
- in_review: submitted for founder review.
- approved: explicit approval recorded with date and evidence.
- superseded: retained for history; do not use for new work.

Every production asset needs an entry in brand/ASSET_REGISTER.csv, including path, version, status, approver, approval date, evidence, and intended use. Do not infer approval from a commit, generated filename, or visual similarity.

## Release checks

Confirm paths resolve, vector contents match their declared format, transparent exports actually contain transparency, mirrored variants are clearly labeled, and required licenses accompany supplied fonts or third-party assets. Check readability at intended size and contrast for learning/UI text. Compare the logo with its approved reference.

Create a versioned release ZIP only after asset approval. Include guidelines, manifest, approved assets, and required licenses; exclude working files and unapproved variants. Record the source commit and release version. Never call a documentation-only scaffold a finished brand kit.
