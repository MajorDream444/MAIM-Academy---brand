ACADEMY CREDENTIALS · DECIDED 2026-11-01
MAIM certifies the mindset. The Academy certifies that the person can perform the work: issued only when all six projects are accepted, all four sessions attended and the showcase delivered (criteria below). Both carry a certificate and a badge; the Academy versions use the Academy crest, square corners and gold. Not an accredited qualification.
Issuing criteria
APPROVED 2026-11-01
1 · ACCEPTED WORK
All six opening-sequence projects (Audit, Design, Prototype, Test, Ship, Measure) reach Accepted. Accepted means every success criterion on the project brief is met; anything less goes back as a revision request.
2 · REVIEW
Peer review first: another cohort member checks the work against the brief and leaves notes. Then the facilitator reviews and decides Accepted or Revision requested. Major Dream Williams signs the certificate.
3 · ATTENDANCE
All four group sessions, live or by replay. Replay counts when the learner watches it and posts one takeaway in the cohort space before the next session.
4 · SHOWCASE
A short final showcase presentation in the week 4 session: the problem, what was built, and the Measure result.
5 · EVIDENCE ON FILE
For each project: the submitted file or link, peer and facilitator feedback, and the accept date. Ship: a working link or recording used by a real person. Measure: one tracked result (time saved, quality, adoption or revenue). Plus the attendance record and the showcase recording.
6 · PUBLIC VERIFICATION
The credential ID resolves to a public page showing the learner’s name, cohort, issue date and the six accepted projects.
COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION · RECOGNISED SEPARATELY
Part of the cohort: each participant personally invites at least one person who could benefit from MAIM Academy (it counts whether or not they enroll) and shares one useful lesson or resource with the community. This earns separate recognition; it does not affect the certificate or badge, which are based on demonstrated work only. Layouts: Community recognition.
