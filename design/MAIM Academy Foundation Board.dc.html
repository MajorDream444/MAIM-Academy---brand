1 · Logo hierarchy

Approved artwork 2026-10-31. Roles, clear space and minimums approved 2026-10-31.
PRIMARY · RASTER
Metallic presentation crest
Hero placements, video, thumbnails, social. Preserve as supplied: no recolor, redraw, effects or crop. Dark grounds only.
Min: 128 px digital. Not for print.
SECONDARY · VECTOR
Simplified flat-color crest
Documents, workbooks, certificates of participation, print, signage. Self-contained black disk works on light or dark grounds.
Min: 64 px digital · 25 mm print.
COMPACT · VECTOR
Small MD mark
Nav bars, avatars, favicons, page footers, watermark corners. Replaces the crest wherever it would render under 64 px.
Min: 32 px digital. 16 px allowed for favicons only.
Clear space
Keep 25% of the mark's diameter clear on every side (40 px at 160 px). Applies to all three versions.
Hierarchy rule
One crest per surface. If the metallic crest is present, the small mark may appear only as a functional icon (nav, favicon).
Placement
Pages: mark top-left in navigation. Media: crest centered or lower-left. Documents: simplified crest on the cover, mark in running footer.
Tri-bar
Green → gold → blue, equal thirds, 3 px tall (4 px at 1920+). Use once per surface as a section or signature mark.

5 · Learning components

Shown on paper (White Clarity), the proposed default for learning. See the lesson example.
OBJECTIVE
"By the end of this lesson you can…" One sentence, one verb.
INSTRUCTIONS
Numbered steps in a gold-ruled list. Max 7 steps per block.
ASSIGNMENT
Black block on paper. Holds task, deliverable, due date (editable).
SUBMISSION
Text answer + file attach + submit. States: draft, submitted (green), needs revision (orange).
FEEDBACK
Reviewer name, date, note. Plain language, one next step.
PROGRESS
Segmented: complete green, current gold, upcoming paper line. Always paired with "3 of 6" text.
